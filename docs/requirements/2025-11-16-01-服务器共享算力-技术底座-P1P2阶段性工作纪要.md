## 监控与 SLO（DeepSeek 专区）
- 指标绑定建议：
  - 请求率：`sum(rate(deepseek_request_total[5m])) by (endpoint)`
  - 错误率：`sum(rate(deepseek_errors_total[5m])) by (endpoint) / sum(rate(deepseek_request_total[5m])) by (endpoint)`
